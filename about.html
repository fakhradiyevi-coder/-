History and Mission

JTEM was founded to address the growing need for rigorous scientific communication between fundamental epigenetic research and its clinical and public health applications. The journal supports transparent, methodologically sound studies that translate molecular findings into diagnostic tools, preventive strategies, and therapeutic interventions.

The journal adheres to established international standards for peer review, editorial independence, and publication ethics. It collaborates with DOI registration services and supports ORCID integration for accurate contributor identification.

Target Audience

The journal serves clinicians, biomedical scientists, epidemiologists, public health researchers, and policy professionals working in translational medicine, molecular diagnostics, and epigenetic epidemiology.
