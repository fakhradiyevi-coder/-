Manuscript Structure

Original research articles should follow IMRAD: Introduction, Methods, Results, and Discussion. Include a title page, structured abstract, main text, references, tables, figures, and any supplementary materials.

Abstracts and Keywords

Provide a structured abstract (Background, Methods, Results, Conclusions) of 250–300 words and 4–6 keywords using MeSH terms when applicable.

Reporting Standards

  • CONSORT for randomized clinical trials
  • PRISMA for systematic reviews and meta-analyses
  • STROBE for observational studies
  • CARE for case reports
  • ARRIVE for animal studies

Completed checklists should be submitted as supplementary files.

Ethics Requirements

  • Ethics committee approval details (institution, protocol number, date).
  • Informed consent statement for human studies.
  • Clinical trial registration number, when applicable.
  • Conflict of interest disclosure for all authors.

Data and Materials

Provide a data availability statement specifying repository links, access conditions, and any restrictions. If data cannot be shared, explain the limitations.

References

References should follow the Vancouver style and be presented in Roman script. Include DOI for all references where available.

Submission Process

  1. Prepare manuscript files according to these instructions.
  2. Submit via the online submission system using the “Submit Manuscript” page.
  3. Confirm ORCID IDs for all authors during submission.
  4. Upload ethics approvals, reporting checklists, and data statements.
  5. Track progress through the editorial system.
