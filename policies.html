Indexing and Metadata Standards

  • Stable URLs for all articles and issue pages.
  • Machine-readable metadata (title, authors, ORCID, affiliations, abstract, keywords).
  • DOI registration and Crossref deposits for each article.
  • Publication dates for received, accepted, and published stages.

Scopus Readiness Checklist

  • Clear journal aims and scope with a consistent medical focus.
  • International editorial board aligned with journal subject area.
  • Transparent peer review and publication ethics policies.
  • Regular publication schedule with documented issue history.
  • Article-level metadata, DOI, and citation-ready references.
  • Author instructions and reporting standards (CONSORT, PRISMA, STROBE).
  • Digital archiving and preservation strategy.
  • No misleading metrics, fake indexes, or undisclosed fees.
