Online Submission

Manuscripts are submitted through the journal’s online system, compatible with OJS or equivalent platforms. Authors must register with an ORCID iD and provide all required disclosures and ethics documentation.

  1. Complete the author profile and link ORCID identifiers.
  2. Upload manuscript files, figures, and supplementary materials.
  3. Provide ethics approvals, trial registration, and data availability statements.
  4. Confirm compliance with reporting standards and reference style.
  5. Submit and track progress in the author dashboard.

Required Metadata

  • Full author names, affiliations, and ORCID iDs.
  • Structured abstract and keywords.
  • Funding statement and conflict of interest declaration.
  • Ethics approval and informed consent details.
