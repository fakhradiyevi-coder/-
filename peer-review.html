Review Model

JTEM uses a double-blind peer review model. Author and reviewer identities are masked to support objective evaluation and reduce bias.

Submission Assessment Workflow

  1. Initial editorial screening (3–7 days): The editorial office checks scope, completeness, ethics documentation, plagiarism screening results, and basic methodological soundness.
  2. Assignment to handling editor (up to 5 days): A section or associate editor evaluates suitability, identifies potential reviewers, and confirms absence of conflicts.
  3. External peer review (21–28 days): At least two independent reviewers provide structured reports on originality, study design, reporting quality, and clinical relevance.
  4. Editorial decision (7–10 days): The handling editor synthesizes reviews and recommends acceptance, revision, or rejection. The Editor-in-Chief confirms the decision.
  5. Revision and verification (14–30 days): Authors submit a point-by-point response. Revised manuscripts may be returned to reviewers when needed.
  6. Final checks and acceptance (5–7 days): Ethics statements, data availability, and reporting checklists are verified before acceptance.

Reviewer Selection Criteria

  • Demonstrated subject-matter expertise and recent publications in relevant fields.
  • Institutional or geographic diversity to ensure balanced perspectives.
  • Absence of conflicts of interest with authors, institutions, or funding sources.

Conflicts of Interest

Reviewers, editors, and authors must disclose potential conflicts of interest. If a conflict is identified, alternative reviewers or editors are assigned. Declarations are recorded in the editorial system.

Appeals Procedure

Authors may submit a reasoned appeal within 30 days of the decision. Appeals are reviewed by the Editor-in-Chief or an independent senior editor who was not involved in the initial decision. Final determinations are communicated in writing with rationale.
