Home

Journal of Translational and Epigenetic Medicine (JTEM) is an international, peer-reviewed scientific journal dedicated to research at the intersection of translational medicine, epigenetics, and precision health. The journal publishes original research and critical analyses that connect molecular and epigenetic mechanisms with clinical practice and population health.

The journal provides an academically rigorous platform for clinicians, biomedical scientists, and public health researchers. All key journal information, policies, and author guidance are available through the navigation above in no more than two clicks.

Technical and Publishing Requirements

  • HTTPS is required for all journal pages and submission workflows.
  • DOI registration and metadata deposits are supported via Crossref.
  • ORCID identifiers are collected for all authors and integrated into metadata.
  • Content is compatible with OJS or comparable journal publishing platforms.
  • Long-term digital preservation is ensured via reputable archiving services.
  • All pages are designed for correct indexing by major search engines.
  • The website avoids aggressive marketing and maintains an academic tone.
