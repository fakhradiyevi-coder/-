Aims

  • Promote translational research in epigenetics, molecular medicine, and precision health.
  • Support evidence-based application of epigenetic biomarkers in clinical and preventive practice.
  • Facilitate dialogue between molecular biology, clinical medicine, and population health.

Scope

The journal considers submissions focused on clinical and biomedical translation of epigenetic research, including:

  • Epigenetic biomarkers of disease, aging, and treatment response.
  • DNA methylation, histone modification, and chromatin remodeling in clinical contexts.
  • Translational epigenomics and precision medicine.
  • Epigenetic epidemiology and population-based studies.
  • Gene–environment interactions with clinical relevance.
  • Clinical validation and implementation of epigenetic assays.
  • Ethical, regulatory, and methodological aspects of epigenetic medicine.

Article Types

  • Original Research Articles
  • Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses
  • Translational Reviews
  • Clinical and Population-Based Studies
  • Methodological Papers
  • Short Communications
  • Editorials and Commentaries
